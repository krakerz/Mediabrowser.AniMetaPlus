Items that keep what they have until a paused source answers again.

Values another source supplied while the preferred one could not be asked. They are replaced once it answers, or wait for review. Keep accepts the stand-in and stops the retries; should the preferred source answer for it later, its value waits on the Review tab. The queue retries the others at most every 6 hours.

Untick TheTVDB and TheMovieDb in the anime library's settings and tick only AniMeta+. They stay installed, and AniMeta+ asks them itself where they are listed below. A provider ticked below AniMeta+ in the library fills fields AniMeta+ left empty, with its own data.

Pause: the item keeps what it has and waits. Switch: the next source fills in, and is replaced once this one answers again. Switch + review: the next source fills in, and the replacement waits for your review. Bans are per IP address. The gap between requests never goes below each source's default.

Source order

The first source is asked first. Drag a source, or use its arrows, to change the order; the cross takes it out and the dashed buttons add one back. Rows marked "combined" take something from every source listed.

Genres and tags

Rebuilds every anime's genres and tags from the answers already cached, and never asks AniDB. It follows the Genres & tags order above and the genre settings on the General tab, and leaves locked fields alone. The two settings are saved with the Save button above.

AniDB

Every program must register its own client on AniDB (anidb.net, your profile, "Client registration"). AniDB is not asked anything while this is empty. A ban is per IP address: a different client name does not lift it.

MyAnimeList

Create a client id at myanimelist.net/apiconfig. Without one, MyAnimeList can still be read through Jikan, which MyAnimeList does not run.

AniList

Needs no key.

The AniDB titles dump is what anime are identified from. It is downloaded by the scheduled task or "Download now", and never more than once every 3 days, counted from the last attempt: AniDB bans a client that downloads it more than once a day.

Unmatched : items nothing could identify. Pick a guess, search the titles dump (Enter), search the titles dump, AniList and MyAnimeList together (one item, or all listed) for anime AniDB does not list yet, or paste an AniDB, AniList or MyAnimeList id or link. One AniDB has no entry for is filled from AniList, MyAnimeList, TVDB and TMDB. "Map" applies on the next queue run; "Map now" refreshes the item at once. A mapped item moves to the Mapping tab, where its hand mapping can be removed.
