AniMeta+ for Emby Server (4.10 or newer)
========================================

1. Copy AniMetaPlus.dll into Emby's plugins folder:
     Linux package:  /var/lib/emby/plugins
     Docker:         /config/plugins
     Windows:        %AppData%\Emby-Server\programdata\plugins
2. Restart Emby.
3. Open Settings > AniMeta+:
   - Clients & keys: enter your own AniDB client name (register one on anidb.net,
     your profile, "Client registration") and press Test. AniDB is not asked
     anything until a client name is set.
   - Optional: a MyAnimeList client id, or turn on Jikan.
4. In your anime library's settings: tick AniMeta+ as metadata and image
   downloader for Series, Season and Episode, and untick TheTVDB and TheMovieDb.
   AniMeta+ still uses them itself where its source order lists them.
5. Optional: put AniDB's anime-titles.dat.gz in
   <plugins>/configurations/AniMetaPlus/ so anime can be identified at once;
   otherwise the "Refresh AniDB titles dump" task downloads it (at most once every 3 days).

Settings, blocks and mappings live in <plugins>/configurations/AniMetaPlus/.
